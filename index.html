CBO, the Chief Breakfast Officer

CBO is a small Django web application that organises team breakfasts fairly: everybody has a points balance, hosts are drawn at random (the lower the balance, the likelier the draw), hosting earns points and attending costs one. Participants and hosts are notified by e-mail with a calendar invitation.

The interface is available in English and French; users switch language from the header.
